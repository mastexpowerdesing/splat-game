Privacy Policy

SPLAT! Photo Splat · Last updated: August 30, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the "SPLAT! Photo Splat" app (the "App") handles user data. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1) What data do we collect?

We never send any of your files to a server. The App processes the photos and music/audio files you select entirely in your device's memory, within the browser. These files are never uploaded to the internet, sent to our servers or any third party's servers, and are never stored.

When the App is closed or the page is refreshed, the photos and music files you selected are cleared from memory; no permanent record is kept.

2) Ads and third-party services

To generate revenue, the App may use Google AdSense / Google Ad Placement (H5 Games Ads). While this service is running, Google may collect data such as cookies, advertising identifiers, and device information to personalize ads, in accordance with its own privacy policy.

  • To learn how Google uses this data, visit: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • To opt out of personalized ads, visit: adssettings.google.com

The App also uses Google Fonts for its typography; this service sends a standard request to Google's servers when the page loads (no personal data is shared beyond technical information such as your IP address).

3) Permissions

The App uses your browser's standard "choose file" dialog to let you select photos and music. This does not grant full access to your device's gallery or files - it only grants access to the specific file you choose, and as noted above, that file never leaves your device.

4) Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. The App has no sign-up or account-creation process of any kind.

5) Data security

Since photo and music files are never sent to any server, this data remains entirely under your own device's security measures.

6) Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised whenever a significant change is made.
